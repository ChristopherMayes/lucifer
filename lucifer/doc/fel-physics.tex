\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

This manual states the physics and the numerical conventions of the Bmad FEL tracker.
The code references these sections by label; the validation harness
(\code{lucifer/tests/}) proves each statement against Genesis 1.3 Version 4, against
closed forms, or against independent implementations, and the measured levels live in
\code{lucifer/README.md}. Each section ends with a \emph{Provenance} note (the Genesis
routine the physics was transcribed from, moved here from the source comments) and a
\emph{Validation} note (which check pins it). The physics was transcribed from Genesis
(GPL permits transcription), validated at transcription level, and then re-expressed in
Bmad's units and conventions; the transcription-era agreement is banked in the README.
Citations of the form FINDINGS.md~n.m (and ``design brief \S x.y'') refer to the
port's development records --- the transcription-findings log and the design brief ---
maintained with the project outside this repository.

Throughout: $m_e c^2$ is Bmad's \code{m\_electron} (eV), $c$ is \code{c\_light},
$Z_0 = \mu_0 c$ (\code{mu\_0\_vac*c\_light}), $e$ is \code{e\_charge}, and
$\varepsilon_0$ is \code{eps\_0\_vac}. Genesis carries its own truncated constants
(notably $Z_0 = 376.73$ against $376.7303\ldots$); the resulting $8.3\times10^{-7}$
relative difference is the accepted floor of every Genesis comparison
(\S\ref{sec:numerics}).

\section{Coordinates and conventions}\label{sec:coords}

\subsection{The packed chart}\label{sec:chart}

Particles are stored in packed structure-of-arrays slices (\code{fel\_slice\_struct}),
in Bmad's phase-space coordinates plus a weight:
\begin{equation}
  \bigl(x,\; p_x/p_0,\; y,\; p_y/p_0,\; z,\; p_z\bigr), \qquad
  w = \text{macroparticle charge [C]},
\end{equation}
with $z = -\beta c\,(t - t_{\mathrm{ref}})$ and $p_z = (P - p_0)/p_0$. The single stored
normalization reference is $p_0 c$ in eV (\code{fel\_beam\_struct\%p0c}); everything
kinematic derives from it exactly (\code{fel\_gamma\_of}, \code{fel\_beta\_of}):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:kinematics}
  \hat P = \frac{p_0}{m_e c}(1+p_z), \qquad
  \gamma = \sqrt{\hat P^2 + 1}, \qquad
  \beta = \hat P/\gamma, \qquad
  \tau \equiv -z/\beta = c\,(t - t_{\mathrm{ref}}).
\end{equation}

The ponderomotive phase is \emph{derived, never stored}. Genesis's per-particle
$\theta$ splits into a common reference advance --- one scalar per beam, $\varphi_0$,
advanced once per step --- and the particle lag carried by Bmad's $z$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:theta}
  \theta_j \;=\; \varphi_0 + k_s\, z_j/\beta_j \;=\; \varphi_0 - k_s\,\tau_j ,
  \qquad k_s = 2\pi/\lambda_s .
\end{equation}
This reference-offset split is the FP32-safe formulation (design brief \S8) and removes
the wrap hazard outright: $z$ does not wrap, so slice migration
(\S\ref{sec:migration}) needs no $\theta$-wrap protocol. The common advance rate over
an element with reference wavenumber $k_u^{\mathrm{like}}$ is computed
cancellation-free (\code{fel\_phi0\_rate}):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phi0rate}
  \frac{d\varphi_0}{ds} = k_u^{\mathrm{like}} + k_s\Bigl(1 - \frac{1}{\beta_0}\Bigr),
  \qquad
  1 - \frac{1}{\beta_0} = \frac{-1}{\beta_0\,\gamma_{0b}^2\,(1+\beta_0)},\quad
  \gamma_{0b}^2 = \hat P_0^2 + 1 .
\end{equation}
Inside undulators $k_u^{\mathrm{like}} = k_u$; over field-free interludes Genesis's
drift surrogate $k_u^{\mathrm{like}} = k_s/(2\gamma_0^2)$ is used
(\S\ref{sec:interlude}).

\subsection{The time window}\label{sec:window}

A time-dependent beam is a set of slices with longitudinal spacing
$\Delta s = \code{sample}\cdot\lambda_s$ (integer \code{sample}). \emph{Higher slice
index is the window head} (earlier arrival, larger Bmad $z$). The global window
position of a particle in slice $i$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:zglobal}
  z_{\mathrm{global}} = z_{\mathrm{local}} + \beta\,(i-1)\,\Delta s ,
\end{equation}
the invariant that slice migration preserves (\S\ref{sec:migration}) and that the
whole-window wake concatenation uses directly (\S\ref{sec:seamwake}). A single-slice
beam is the steady state: slippage is a no-op and the window degenerates to one
$\lambda_s$-periodic slice.

\subsection{Units}\label{sec:units}

The radiation field is stored in V/m (the \code{wavefront\_struct} convention); there
is no internal unit system. Beam energies in diagnostics are total energies
$E = \gamma\, m_e c^2$ in eV (Bmad's convention --- \emph{energy never means}
$\gamma$; Genesis's \code{energy} output \emph{is} $\gamma$, and the comparison
scripts convert). Genesis's internal field unit $u$ relates to the physical field by
$u = E\,k_s/(\sqrt2\, m_e c^2)$, the identity under which every formula in this manual
was converted from its internal-unit original; the converted forms are simpler
(\S\ref{sec:eom}, \S\ref{sec:field}).

\begin{provenance}
The chart and window conventions are this port's own (design brief \S4.2, \S8);
Genesis stores $(\theta,\gamma)$ per particle with per-slice bounded $\theta$. The
unit identity composes Genesis's dump scale (\code{writeFieldHDF5.cpp}) with
$E = \mathit{dfl}\,\sqrt{2 Z_0}/\mathit{dgrid}$.
\end{provenance}
\begin{validation}
The chart is exercised by every tier; the theta identity specifically by the
bit-identity of the shot-noise-off loader against the deliverable-4 baseline, and
Eq.~\ref{eq:zglobal} by the migration phase-continuity check ($6.9\times10^{-15}$) and
the seam-wake causality check (exactly zero ahead of all charge).
\end{validation}

\section{The FEL element}\label{sec:element}

An FEL segment is a Bmad \code{wiggler}/\code{undulator} with
\code{tracking\_method = custom}, recognized by key and method, never by name. Its FEL
parameters derive from lattice attributes:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:awmap}
  K = \frac{c\,B_{\max}}{k_u\, m_e c^2/e c} = \frac{c\,B_{\max}}{k_u}\frac{e}{m_e c},
  \qquad
  a_w = \begin{cases} K & \text{helical (\code{field\_calc = helical\_model})}\\
                      K/\sqrt2 & \text{planar (\code{planar\_model})}\end{cases}
\end{equation}
($a_w$ is rms, Genesis's convention; in code $K = c\,\code{b\_max}/(k_u\,
\code{m\_electron})$ with \code{b\_max} in Tesla and \code{m\_electron} in eV). The
natural-focusing split follows the helicity defaults, scaled by $k_u^2$:
helical $k_x = k_y = \tfrac12 k_u^2$; planar $k_x = 0$, $k_y = k_u^2$. The integration
step is the element's own \code{ds\_step}/\code{num\_steps}; the bookkeeper's
$n_{\mathrm{step}} = \max(1, \mathrm{nint}(L/\code{ds\_step}))$ is exactly Genesis's
unroll.

The FEL tracking mode and unaveraged parameters are likewise per-element lattice
attributes (registered program-side; class-settable as
\code{wiggler::*[attr] = ...}), so averaged and unaveraged segments mix freely in one
line. Lattices use NAMED values via one-line variable definitions
(\code{fel\_unaveraged = 1}, matching the code's \code{fel\_unaveraged\$}-style
parameters): \code{fel\_tracking} --- unset/0 (\code{fel\_averaged}) the averaged
default with the
\code{bmad\_standard} kernel's transverse maps (\S\ref{sec:undbmad}); 1 the
unaveraged mode (\S\ref{sec:unaveraged}); $-1$ the transcribed-Genesis maps,
validation-internal --- \code{fel\_steps\_per\_period} (unset $\to$ 20; below 10
refused), and \code{fel\_ramp\_periods} (unset $\to$ 2). An attribute's unset value
is 0 and a silent hard edge would reintroduce the $K/\gamma$ handoff hazard, so a
true hard edge --- the test configuration --- must be asked for by name with the
sentinel $-1$; silence never means hard edge. The ramps carry two priced end
effects (\S\ref{sec:unaveraged}): their slippage deficit is compensated exactly by
the mode's built-in end-of-segment phase jump, and their reduced coupling length
costs $\sim$2\% in $\ln P$ per 266-period benchmark segment at the default 2
periods --- real field physics of adiabatic ends, scaling with the ramp length,
deliberately left visible. \code{tilt} is honored on planar elements as the polarization specification
(\S\ref{sec:vector}): the wiggle plane, the focusing, the coupling roll-off and the
unaveraged field all rotate with it, and the radiation grows a second component.
Refused by name at parse time: missing \code{b\_max} or \code{l\_period}, a
fieldmap \code{field\_calc}, nonzero \code{kx} (unmapped roll-off), an unset
\code{ds\_step}, \code{tilt} on a helical element (a rotation of a circularly
symmetric field --- a no-op that reads as a mistake) and \code{tilt} with the
transcribed-Genesis maps (Genesis has no tilt). Outside the FEL walk the element is a plain periodic wiggler: the
\code{track1\_custom} and \code{make\_mat6\_custom} hooks delegate to
\code{track\_a\_wiggler}, so reference time and transfer matrices come from Bmad's own
kernel.

\begin{provenance}
Helicity defaults from Genesis's \code{LatticeParser} ($k_x{=}k_y{=}0.5$ helical,
$0/1$ planar, scaled by $k_u^2$ in the unroll); the step rule from
\code{Lattice::unrollLattice}.
\end{provenance}
\begin{validation}
The element-driven line reproduces the namelist-driven reference bit-identically at
$z=0$, growing to $3.4\times10^{-12}$ (the 1-ulp $a_w$ attribute round trip); the
assertion checks refuse each malformed lattice by name; mutations (rms factor, wrong
helicity attribute) fail tier1 at $\sim$1.0 relative.
\end{validation}

\section{The FEL core}\label{sec:core}

\subsection{Step composition}\label{sec:step}

One integration step of length $\delta z$ inside an undulator advances, in order
(\code{fel\_track\_und\_step}): (i) transverse half step; (ii) longitudinal RK4 with
the field gathered once and held; (iii) the wake's energy decrement
(\S\ref{sec:wakes}); (iv) transverse half step; (v) source deposition and field
propagation (\S\ref{sec:field}); (vi) slippage, applied by the caller
(\S\ref{sec:slippage}). $\varphi_0$ advances once per step by Eq.~\ref{eq:phi0rate}
with $k_u^{\mathrm{like}} = k_u$. Beam slice $i$ couples to field record slice
$1 + \mathrm{mod}(i-1+\code{first},\, n_{\mathrm{slice}})$ (\S\ref{sec:slippage}).

\subsection{Coupling and field roll-off}\label{sec:coupling}

The coupling factor at harmonic $h$ (\code{fel\_und\_coupling}):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fc}
  f_c(h) = \begin{cases}
    a_w & \text{helical, } h=1; \quad 0 \text{ otherwise},\\[2pt]
    a_w\,(-1)^{(h-1)/2}\Bigl[J_{\frac{h-1}{2}}(\xi) - J_{\frac{h+1}{2}}(\xi)\Bigr],
    \;\; \xi = \frac{h}{2}\frac{a_w^2}{1+a_w^2} & \text{planar, odd } h;\quad 0 \text{ even.}
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}
Only $h=1$ is used (harmonics beyond the coupling formula are out of scope). The
transverse roll-off of the undulator field about its offset $(a_x, a_y)$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:faw}
  f_{aw}(x,y) = 1 + \tfrac12\!\left[k_x (x-a_x)^2 + k_y (y-a_y)^2\right], \qquad
  f_{aw}^2(x,y) = 1 + k_x (x-a_x)^2 + k_y (y-a_y)^2 ,
\end{equation}
the first-order form in the particle gather, the squared form in the deposition.

\subsection{Longitudinal equations of motion}\label{sec:eom}

Per particle, with $\hat p_{x,y} = \gamma\beta_{x,y}$ (in code
$p_{x}\cdot p_0/m_ec$),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:btpar}
  b_\perp = 1 + \hat p_x^2 + \hat p_y^2 + a_w^2 f_{aw}^2(x,y),
\end{equation}
and the complex drive from the local field $E$ (bilinear interpolation from the
particle's cell, zero off-grid),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rpart}
  r = \frac{f_c(1)}{\sqrt2\, m_e c^2}\, f_{aw}(x,y)\, E^{*} \quad [\mathrm{m}^{-1}],
  \qquad \hat c = r\, e^{-i\theta},
\end{equation}
the equations of motion integrated by RK4 (\code{fel\_ode}) are
\begin{align}
  \beta_\parallel &= \sqrt{1 - \frac{b_\perp - (2/k_s)\,\mathrm{Re}\,\hat c}{\gamma^2}},
  \label{eq:betapar}\\
  \frac{d\theta}{dz} &= k_s\Bigl(1 - \frac{1}{\beta_\parallel}\Bigr) + k_u,
  \label{eq:dtheta}\\
  \frac{d\gamma}{dz} &= \frac{\mathrm{Im}\,\hat c}{\beta_\parallel\,\gamma} - e_z,
  \label{eq:dgamma}
\end{align}
with $e_z$ the space-charge term (\S\ref{sec:spacecharge}) in units of $m_e c^2$ per
meter, held fixed through the stages along with $r$, $\hat p_{x,y}$ and $f_{aw}$. The
integrator (\code{fel\_runge\_kutta}) is the classical fourth-order Runge--Kutta in
Genesis's incremental bookkeeping, kept verbatim so the audited transcription
survives.

\emph{Chart round trip.} $(\theta,\gamma)$ are derived at step entry from
Eqs.~\ref{eq:kinematics}--\ref{eq:theta} and written back at exit:
$\hat P = \sqrt{\gamma^2-1}$, $p_z = (\hat P m_e c - p_0)/p_0$ (exact once $\hat P$ is
formed), $z = -\beta\,(\varphi_0^{\mathrm{new}} - \theta)/k_s$ with the updated
$\beta$. The $\theta \leftrightarrow z$ map is affine with a state-independent shift,
under which RK4 is exactly invariant; the $\gamma \leftrightarrow p_z$ round trip
costs $\sim$1 ulp of $\gamma$ per step (\S\ref{sec:numerics}).

\begin{provenance}
\code{BeamSolver::advance} (gather, $r$, stage order), \code{BeamSolver::RungeKutta}
(verbatim), \code{BeamSolver::ODE}; \code{Undulator::fc}, \code{faw}, \code{faw2};
grid weights from \code{Field::getLLGridpoint}. The V/m forms follow from the unit
identity of \S\ref{sec:units}, under which the rest energy and $k_s$ cancel out of
$r$.
\end{provenance}
\begin{validation}
tier1 (one segment, FEL core alone): $1.8\times10^{-6}$, the constants floor
(transcription-era $2.8\times10^{-11}$ with Genesis's constants). Source-term and
conjugation mutations fail tier1 at $3.5\times10^{-1}$ and $2.8\times10^{-2}$.
\end{validation}

\section{Transverse motion}\label{sec:transverse}

\subsection{Natural focusing inside undulators}\label{sec:natfocus}

The default in-undulator transverse transport (\code{fel\_transverse\_track}) is a
drift-plus-natural-focusing map over each half step. With the per-particle
longitudinal factor in stored normalization,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gzhat}
  \hat\gamma_z = \gamma_z\frac{m_ec}{p_0} = \sqrt{(1+p_z)^2 - (p_x/p_0)^2 - (p_y/p_0)^2
   - (a_w m_ec/p_0)^2},
\end{equation}
the effective focusing strengths are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:qnat}
  \hat q_{x,y} = \frac{k_{x,y}\, a_w^2}{\gamma_0\,\beta_{\parallel 0}}\frac{m_ec}{p_0},
  \qquad
  \beta_{\parallel 0} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{1 + a_w^2}{\gamma_0^2}},
\end{equation}
and each plane maps by (\code{fel\_apply\_focus}; $\Omega = \sqrt{\hat q/\hat\gamma_z}\,
\delta z$)
\begin{equation}\label{eq:focusmap}
  \begin{pmatrix} x \\ p_x/p_0 \end{pmatrix} \mapsto
  \begin{pmatrix} \cos\Omega & \dfrac{\sin\Omega}{\sqrt{\hat q \hat\gamma_z}} \\[6pt]
    -\sqrt{\hat q\hat\gamma_z}\,\sin\Omega & \cos\Omega \end{pmatrix}
  \begin{pmatrix} x \\ p_x/p_0 \end{pmatrix},
\end{equation}
with $\cosh/\sinh$ for $\hat q<0$ and the plain drift $x \mathrel{+}= p_x\,\delta z/
(p_0\hat\gamma_z)$ for $\hat q = 0$. Chromaticity is through $\hat\gamma_z$
(Genesis's $\gamma_z$ scaling).

\subsection{The Genesis interlude model}\label{sec:interlude}

Field-free elements (drifts, quadrupoles) tracked ``Genesis's way'' are one
integration step of the full element length: transverse half (quad strength
$\hat q = k_1\gamma_0\, m_ec/p_0$, Genesis's chromatic convention $k_1\gamma_0/
\gamma_z$ against Bmad's $k_1 p_0/P$), then the $\theta$ advance with the path-length
term sampled once at mid element,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:interludetheta}
  \Delta\theta = L\left[k_s\Bigl(1-\frac{1}{\beta_{\parallel}}\Bigr)
   + \frac{k_s}{2\gamma_0^2}\right],\qquad
  \beta_\parallel = \sqrt{1 - \frac{1 + \hat p_x^2 + \hat p_y^2}{\gamma^2}},
\end{equation}
then the wake decrement and the second transverse half. With space charge off the RK4
collapses exactly (the slope is $\theta$-independent and $\gamma$ constant) and the
collapsed step is kept for bit-identity; with space charge on the full RK4 runs with
$r=0$ and the per-particle $e_z$. This model exists to \emph{price} what the Bmad seam
changes: the seam integrates the path-length term exactly through the quad map where
this samples it once --- a measured $\sim\!2\times10^{-3}$ rad of bunching phase per
quadrupole, compounding to the documented tier2\_bmad difference.

\subsection{The Bmad-kernel default}\label{sec:undbmad}

The DEFAULT transverse maps (\code{fel\_tracking} unset; \S\ref{sec:element}) are
Bmad's own: the \code{bmad\_standard} periodic-wiggler kernel, flattened to per-step
granularity. The transcribed maps of \S\ref{sec:natfocus} remain selectable as
\code{fel\_tracking = -1}, validation-internal --- the Genesis comparisons require
transcription-level transport; no production lattice writes it. The kernel, per
particle: $k_1 = -\tfrac12 g_{\max}^2/
(1+p_z)^2$ with $g_{\max} = K k_u\,m_ec/p_0$ ($K$ from Eq.~\ref{eq:awmap}), quadrupole
bodies via \code{quad\_mat2\_calc}, and half-octupole edge kicks
$\delta p_y = k_3 L\,(1{+}p_z)\, k_u^2\, y^3/3$ (and $x$ for helical) with
$k_3 L = 2\,\delta z\, k_1$. The kernel's own $z$ bookkeeping is deliberately absent
--- the phase evolution lives entirely in \S\ref{sec:eom}, and applying it here too
would double count. Model differences from \S\ref{sec:natfocus} (chromaticity
$1/(1+p_z)^2$ vs $1/\hat\gamma_z$; the octupole edges) are priced, not argued: power
$5\times10^{-5}$, intensity $7.3\times10^{-4}$, $+3.8\%$ runtime over the full TD
line.

\begin{provenance}
\S\ref{sec:natfocus}--\ref{sec:interlude}: \code{TrackBeam::track},
\code{applyDrift}/\code{applyFQuad}/\code{applyDQuad}, and \code{BeamSolver::advance}'s
drift path with the division order kept. \S\ref{sec:undbmad}:
\code{track\_a\_wiggler.f90} tracking locals (never the stored \code{k1x}/\code{k1y}
attributes, whose helical sign disagrees).
\end{provenance}
\begin{validation}
tier2\_genesis $1.8\times10^{-5}$ (transcribed interludes, constants floor);
tier2\_bmad $5.0\times10^{-2}$, the located and priced transport difference; the
transverse-map pricing above.
\end{validation}

\section{The field solver}\label{sec:field}

Each field slice advances by split-step Fourier propagation with source addition
(\code{fel\_field\_step}). The paraxial kernel, built once per grid
(\code{fel\_field\_kernel\_init}) with $\Delta k = 2\pi/(N\,\mathit{dgrid})$ and
fftshift index mapping,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:k2}
  K_2(k_x,k_y) = -\,i\,\frac{k_x^2 + k_y^2}{2 k_s}, \qquad
  \tilde E \mapsto \tilde E\, e^{K_2\,\delta z},
\end{equation}
followed by the inverse transform and the source added in real space with the factor
2. The source deposits per particle with bilinear weights at
$(\sin\theta + i\cos\theta)\cdot p_j$, where
\begin{equation}\label{eq:source}
  p_j = \sqrt{f_{aw}^2(x_j,y_j)}\;\frac{s_w\, w_j}{\gamma_j}, \qquad
  s_w = \frac{f_c(1)\, Z_0\, \sqrt2\, c\;\delta z}{4\,\mathit{dgrid}^2\,\Delta s} ,
\end{equation}
the weighted V/m form in which the rest energy and $k_s$ have cancelled; Genesis's
$\mathit{current}/n_{\mathrm{part}}$ is the uniform-weight special case of
$c\,w_j/\Delta s$. Field diagnostics: power $= \sum_{\mathrm{cells}}|E|^2\,
\mathit{dgrid}^2/(2Z_0)$; on-axis intensity $=|E(0,0)|^2/(2Z_0)$; the window field
energy plotted by the tools is $U = \sum_i P_i\,\Delta s/c$.

\begin{provenance}
\code{FieldSolverFFT::advance}, \code{FFT}, \code{init} (unfiltered path);
diagnostics per \code{DiagField::calc}. The internal-unit source scale
$f_c\,\mathit{vacimp}\,I\,k_s\,\delta z/(4\,\mathit{eev}\,n\,\mathit{dgrid}^2)$
converts to Eq.~\ref{eq:source} by \S\ref{sec:units}.
\end{provenance}
\begin{validation}
tier1/td1 at the constants floor; the split-weight check ($3.6\times10^{-13}$) pins the
weighted deposition; the dropped-factor-2 mutation fails tier1 at $3.5\times10^{-1}$.
\end{validation}

\section{Time dependence and slippage}\label{sec:slippage}

The field record is a circular buffer over the wavefront's slice index, rotated by
slippage rather than moved. Beam slice $i$ couples to record slice
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fieldindex}
  \mathrm{ifld}(i) = 1 + \mathrm{mod}(i - 1 + \code{first},\, n_{\mathrm{slice}}),
\end{equation}
and everything reading the record in time order (diagnostics, dumps) unrotates through
the same map. Slippage accumulates in units of $\lambda_s$; each undulator step
contributes
\begin{equation}\label{eq:slipstep}
  \delta_{\mathrm{slip}} = \frac{\delta z\,(1 + a_w^2)}{2\gamma_0^2\,\lambda_s},
\end{equation}
and whenever $|\mathrm{accuslip}| > 0.8\cdot\code{sample}$ the record rotates one
slice: the slice coupled to the window \emph{head} is zeroed and re-enters at the tail
--- radiation leaves at the head, fresh vacuum enters behind the bunch (non-periodic
fill; backward slippage mirrors the direction). Interludes slip nothing per step;
instead their lengths $L_z$ accumulate and, when an undulator follows, the last
interlude element receives $\lfloor L_z/(2\gamma_0^2\lambda_s)\rfloor + 1$ wavelengths
of autophasing. The end-of-lattice fixup is \emph{unguarded}: the final element always
receives the same $+1$, even with no trailing interlude at all --- a Genesis quirk
kept deliberately, since guarding it leaves the record one rotation short.

\begin{provenance}
\code{Control::applySlippage} reduced to one shared-memory node (the MPI ring exchange
is the identity); the schedule from \code{Lattice::calcSlippage}, including the
unguarded fixup (found live: the guarded version failed td1 at 0.84 of the final
field).
\end{provenance}
\begin{validation}
td1 $8.5\times10^{-7}$; slippage mutations (never-firing rotation, wrong zeroed slice,
dropped autophasing) fail at $10^{10}$, $10^{9}$, $1.9\times10^{8}$ on the elementwise
per-slice power comparison.
\end{validation}

\section{Loading}\label{sec:loading}

\subsection{Quiet start}\label{sec:quiet}

\emph{One description, two generation methods.} The beam is described by Bmad's
standard \code{beam\_init} structure in both generated paths: the import
(\S\ref{sec:import}) resamples real particles from it; the quiet-start loader here
evaluates it \emph{analytically} per slice. The loader honors
\code{n\_particle} (per slice), \code{a\_norm\_emit}/\code{b\_norm\_emit},
\code{sig\_pz} ($\delta\gamma = \beta_0 p_0 \,\code{sig\_pz}/mc$),
\code{bunch\_charge}, \code{sig\_z} and \code{distribution\_type(3)}, and REFUSES BY
NAME every other field that is set --- a standard structure that silently dropped
fields would be worse than a custom one. The Twiss is always the lattice's
(\S\ref{sec:element}'s one-truth rule), and the current is \emph{derived}, never
input:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:derivedcurrent}
  I(s_i) = \frac{Q c}{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sigma_z}\, e^{-s_i^2/2\sigma_z^2}
  \;\;\text{(Gaussian)}, \qquad
  I = \frac{Q c}{x_{\max}-x_{\min}} \;\;\text{(GRID: Bmad's uniform, flat)},
\end{equation}
evaluated at the slice centers with the bunch centered in the window; $\sigma_z = 0$
is the steady state --- the whole charge in one slice window, $I = Qc/\Delta s$ ---
and is refused by name for time-dependent windows. The default window covers the
described bunch ($\pm4\sigma_z$ Gaussian; the grid extent flat), exactly as the
import derives its window from real particles; \code{window\_length} overrides it
for slippage headroom and warns with numbers when it clips the bunch.

Generated slices load $m = n_{\mathrm{part}}/n_{\mathrm{bins}}$ beamlets: each beamlet
draws one 4D transverse sample and one energy sample, replicated at $n_{\mathrm{bins}}$
phases $\theta = \theta_0 + j\,2\pi/n_{\mathrm{bins}}$, so every bunching harmonic
below $n_{\mathrm{bins}}$ cancels to roundoff. Weights are uniform within a beamlet
(the cancellation is per beamlet).

\subsection{Weighted shot noise}\label{sec:noise}

Physical noise is imposed Fawley-style (\code{fel\_fawley\_noise}), the one-substitution
weighted generalization: the per-beamlet amplitude draws on the beamlet's \emph{real}
electron count, its charge over $e$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fawley}
  \theta_{bj} \mathrel{-}= \sum_{h=0}^{n_h-1} a_{bh}\,
     \sin\!\bigl((h{+}1)\theta_{bj} + \phi_{bh}\bigr),
  \qquad
  a_{bh} = \frac{2}{h+1}\sqrt{\frac{-\ln u_{bh}}{n_{bl}}},
  \quad n_{bl} = \frac{n_{\mathrm{bins}}\, w_b}{e},
\end{equation}
with $n_h = (n_{\mathrm{bins}}-1)/2$, $\phi_{bh}$ uniform in $[0,2\pi)$, and kicks
accumulated from the unperturbed phases. This makes $\langle|b(h)|^2\rangle =
1/N_\lambda$ exact for any cross-beamlet weight distribution, $N_\lambda$ the slice's
real electron count. Genesis's silent $n_{bl}<1$ clamp is kept but counted and warned.
The $N_{\mathrm{eff}}$ guard refuses to impose noise when the pre-noise quiet floor
$\max_h |b(h)|^2$, swept over \emph{every} harmonic the beamlet structure resolves
($h = 1..n_{\mathrm{bins}}-1$), is not far below the target $1/N_\lambda$.

\begin{provenance}
\code{ShotNoise::applyShotNoise}, generalized by $n_{bl} = n_e/m \to n_{\mathrm{bins}}
w_b/e$ (identical for uniform weights). Shared code between the generator and the
distribution import.
\end{provenance}

$0.25\times/1.75\times$ beamlet weights; cross-loader SASE startup to 0.3\%;
mutations at 13--20$\times$ (macroparticle-count amplitudes) and 1.52

\section{Distribution import}\label{sec:import}

An arbitrary \code{bunch\_struct} (from \code{beam\_init} generation or an
openPMD-beamphysics file) resamples into slices by Genesis's method
(\code{fel\_import\_mod}). Window positions are $\tau$ of Eq.~\ref{eq:kinematics},
min-shifted to zero; slice centers sit at $(i-1)\Delta s$;
$n_{\mathrm{slice}} = \mathrm{nint}(\tau_{\mathrm{total}}/\Delta s)$ when not given.
Per slice: every particle inside a sampling window $d s_{\mathrm{len}} =
\code{slicewidth}\cdot\tau_{\mathrm{total}}$ centered on the slice (strict
inequalities) is a candidate, and the slice current comes from the same window,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:importcurrent}
  I_i = \frac{c\sum_{j\in\mathrm{window}} w_j}{d s_{\mathrm{len}}},
\end{equation}
the weighted generalization of Genesis's count$\cdot dQ$. The candidate set reduces to
$n_{\mathrm{part}}/n_{\mathrm{bins}}$ seeds by random deletion, or grows by Genesis's
phase-space interpolation: normalize the five coordinates $(\gamma,x,y,\hat p_x,
\hat p_y)$ to zero mean and unit rms, pick a random parent, find its nearest
\emph{original} neighbor under a metric whose per-coordinate weights are fresh random
draws, and place the child at the midpoint plus $\mathrm{uniform}[-1,1]$ times the
difference per coordinate. $\theta$ is refilled uniformly over one beamlet spacing,
mirrored into $n_{\mathrm{bins}}$, and \S\ref{sec:noise} imposes the noise with
$n_e = \mathrm{nint}(I\lambda_s\,\code{sample}/(e c))$. The file's $p_x/p_y$ are
\emph{slopes}; the slope-to-momentum conversion $\hat p = x'\gamma$ happens at the
copy into the candidate set. Genesis's \code{match}/\code{center} transforms are not
ported (a Bmad lattice carries its optics; \code{init\_beam\_distribution} generates
matched bunches), and Genesis's \code{align*} and \code{shotnoise} inputs are parsed
but dead in v4 --- neither is transcribed as functional. A zero-charge bunch is
refused by name.

\section{Slice migration}\label{sec:migration}

\section{Collective effects}\label{sec:collective}

\subsection{Wake kernels}\label{sec:wakes}

Wakes act as a per-slice energy-loss rate built from three single-particle kernels at
wavelength resolution over the window, $w(s_i)$, $s_i = (i-1)\lambda_s$, in eV per
meter per electron (negative = loss).

\emph{Resistive wall} (\code{fel\_resistive\_wall\_wake}), the numerical impedance of
Bane \& Stupakov (SLAC-PUB-10707) with AC (Drude) conductivity and Leontovich surface
impedance:
\begin{gather}
  \sigma(k) = \frac{\sigma_0}{1 - i k c\tau}, \qquad
  \zeta(k) = (1-i)\sqrt{\frac{k}{2\sigma(k) Z_0}}, \notag\\[2pt]
  Z_{\mathrm{round}}(k) = \frac{Z_0/(2\pi a)}{1/\zeta - i k a/2}, \qquad
  Z_{\mathrm{flat}}(k) = \int_0^{X}\!
     \frac{Z_0/(2\pi a)\; dx}{\cosh x\,[\cosh x/\zeta - i k a\,\mathrm{sinhc}\,x]},
     \label{eq:bszk}\\[2pt]
  w(s) = -e\,\frac{2c}{\pi}\int_0^{k_{\max}} \mathrm{Re}\,Z(k)\cos(k s)\, dk,
  \label{eq:bsws}
\end{gather}
with Genesis's exact numerics kept: $k \in [0, 100/s_0]$ on 1000 trapezoid intervals,
$s_0 = (2a^2/(Z_0\sigma_0))^{1/3}$, flat integral on $[0,15]$ with 20000 points. This
routine is deliberately separable (a future Bmad-proper wake source; \S\ref{sec:seamwake}
already feeds it to Bmad's machinery). \emph{Geometric} (undulator gap, convolved with
$dI/ds$): $w(s) = -\frac{Z_0 c\, e}{\pi^2 a\, L_{\mathrm{gap}}}\,2\sqrt{g/2}\,\sqrt{s}$
($\times0.956$ flat). \emph{Roughness}: $w(s) = \frac{r_r}{\pi}\frac{4}{a^2}
\frac{e}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\,\mathrm{Re}\!\int e^{-iq\tau} K(q)\,dq$ over Genesis's
four-segment complex-$q$ contour, $r_r = \pi^3 h^2 a/L_r^3$, $\tau = 2\pi s/L_r$. The
$s{=}0$ bin of every kernel carries half weight (the self-loading theorem).

\emph{Convolution and application} (\code{fel\_wake\_update}, \code{fel\_wake\_apply}):
slice currents interpolate to $\lambda_s$ resolution with a zero pad past the head
(the trapezoidal density model whose half-slice head deficit is the derived bound of
the seam-wake cross-validation), convert to electrons per bin, and each slice sums
causally \emph{toward the head} --- a trailing slice collects the wake of the charge
ahead --- averaged over the \code{sample} sub-steps:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:eloss}
  \mathcal{E}_i = \frac{1}{\code{sample}}\sum_{j=0}^{\code{sample}-1}
    \sum_{k\ge0} \Bigl[N_{i,j+k}\,\bigl(w_{\mathrm{res}}+w_{\mathrm{rou}}\bigr)_k
     + N'_{i,j+k}\, w_{\mathrm{geo},k}\Bigr] + \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{ext}} .
\end{equation}
The kick is uniform within a slice: every particle loses $\Delta\gamma =
\mathcal{E}_i\,\delta z/(m_e c^2)$ per step, in every element (the chamber does not
end where the undulator does), with $z$ rescaled by $\beta_{\mathrm{new}}/
\beta_{\mathrm{old}}$ to hold $\theta$ fixed through the kick. The convolution is
hoisted while currents cannot change and recomputed at the migration stride.

\subsection{Space charge}\label{sec:spacecharge}

\emph{Short range} (\code{fel\_shortrange\_ez}), per slice: center and radially bin the
particles, then for azimuthal modes $m = -n_\phi..n_\phi$ and longitudinal harmonics
$l = 1..n_z$ solve the radial tridiagonal system of the harmonic potential
(cell volumes $V_1 = \pi\,dr^2$, $V_i = \pi dr^2(2i-1)$; off-diagonals
$2\pi(i-1)$; $\log$ ring terms $-2\pi m^2\ln\frac{i}{i-1}$; outer boundary
$-2\pi N$), with the weighted source per particle
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scsource}
  \frac{Z_0}{m_ec^2}\,\frac{c\, w_j}{\Delta s}\,\frac{1}{k_s}\;
  e^{-im\phi_j}\, e^{-il\theta_j},
\end{equation}
and the operator scale $-\gamma_z^2/k_s^2$ ($\gamma_z^2 = \gamma_0^2/(1+a_w^2)$ inside
undulators, $\gamma_0^2$ in drifts). Each particle receives
$e_z \mathrel{+}= 2\,\mathrm{Re}\bigl[e^{im\phi}e^{il\theta} u_m^l(r)\bigr]$, in
$m_ec^2$ per meter, entering Eq.~\ref{eq:dgamma}. \emph{Long range}
(\code{fel\_longrange\_esc}), whole window, one node:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:longrange}
  E_i = \frac{\Delta s}{2\pi c\,\varepsilon_0}\sum_{j\ne i}
    \mathrm{sgn}(i-j)\,
    \Bigl[1 - \frac{|d_{ij}|}{\sqrt{d_{ij}^2 + \sigma_{x,j}\sigma_{y,j}}}\Bigr]
    \frac{I_j}{\sigma_{x,j}\sigma_{y,j}}, \qquad d_{ij} = (j-i)\,\Delta s\,\gamma_z,
\end{equation}
where the size scale is the \emph{product} $\sigma_x\sigma_y$ (an effective area, not
a variance sum --- transcribed wrong once, caught at $1.7\times10^{-1}$). The
per-particle ODE term is $e_z^{\mathrm{short}}(j) - E_i/(m_ec^2)$. Space charge is
transcribed for consistency; a Bmad-slice implementation behind the same interface is
an explicit future task.

\section{The unaveraged verification mode}\label{sec:unaveraged}

Every input of the averaged map --- the coupling $f_c(h)$, the roll-off $f_{aw}$, the
entry/exit behavior --- is an output of the underlying unaveraged dynamics. This mode
(\code{fel\_unaveraged\_mod}, selected per element by the lattice attribute
\code{fel\_tracking = 1}; \S\ref{sec:element}) integrates the
particles through the undulator's \emph{real} field with the radiation as a
co-evolving kick, so those inputs become measurements. It keeps the grid field and
the Lorentz force where MINERVA --- the production existence proof of this physics ---
uses modal fields; it is a verification mode whose $\sim$30$\times$ cost per step is
priced, not hidden. Because the kick/deposit pair resolves each particle's full quiver
current, a beam carrying \emph{physical shot noise} radiates it continuously into the
SVEA band and pays for it (self-interaction back-reaction) --- spontaneous-emission
physics the period-averaged model does not track (it injects shot noise once, at load
time). On the saturation demo this lifts the unaveraged SASE curve $\sim$2\%/m above
the averaged codes and deepens the beam's energy loss; the effect is independent of
particle count and steps per period (physics, not statistics or resolution), and
quiet-start probes are blind to it by design (the beamlet loading cancels shot noise).
Its absolute calibration against the analytic undulator spectrum is the named future
check (FINDINGS 7.27). Like the averaged path it is parallel over slices with
bit-identical results at any thread count (per-slice private state, fixed-order
energy reduction; the harness runs a 1-thread/8-thread byte comparison). Nothing
from the averaged coupling path may
appear in it (a harness grep enforces this): its agreement with
Eq.~\ref{eq:fc} is the point, not the input.

\emph{The step.} Per substep $\delta$ ($\lambda_u/20$ by default; MINERVA's envelope
is 10--30 per period), a Strang split: half magnetic push, radiation kick and source
deposit at the midpoint, half magnetic push, then the shared pure diffraction
(Eq.~\ref{eq:k2}) and the $+2\cdot$source convention of \S\ref{sec:field}. The
magnetic push is classical RK4 on the exact $z$-ODEs in kinetic variables
$u = \gamma\beta$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unavgode}
  \frac{du_x}{ds} = b_y - \frac{u_y b_z}{u_s},\quad
  \frac{du_y}{ds} = -b_x + \frac{u_x b_z}{u_s},\quad
  \frac{d\tau}{ds} = \frac{\gamma}{u_s} - \frac{1}{\beta_0},\quad
  u_s = \sqrt{\gamma^2 - 1 - u_\perp^2},
\end{equation}
with $\gamma$ exact ($B$ does no work; $\gamma$ changes only in the kick), through
$b = \nabla\times a$, $a = eA/m_ec$:
planar $a_x = a_0\, g(s) \cos(k_u s)\cosh(k_u y)$, $a_0 = \sqrt2\,a_w$; helical
$(a_x, a_y) = a_0\, g(s)(\cos, \sin)(k_u s)\,(1 + k_u^2 r^2/4)$, $a_0 = a_w$ ---
near-axis models whose ponderomotive-average focusing reproduces
\S\ref{sec:natfocus}'s split exactly. The envelope $g(s)$ is a $\sin^2$ ramp over
$n_{\mathrm{ramp}}$ periods at each end, continuous in amplitude \emph{and} slope
with the $g'$ terms retained in $b$: the quiver builds adiabatically and vanishes at
the segment ends, which is where the averaged and unaveraged momentum conventions
coincide. The beam carries a convention flag (\code{quiver\_in\_px}), set inside the
region and asserted by every averaged-physics and seam entry: a hard-edge handoff
injects $K/\gamma$ of spurious transverse momentum (brief 6.6).

\emph{Kick and source.} With the optical phase $\Psi = (\varphi_0 - k_u s) - k_s\tau$
(the reference $\varphi_0$ advances at the averaged rate of \S\ref{sec:chart}, so
every diagnostic stays comparable; the $k_u s$ term is undone because the particles
now actually ride $\cos k_u s$), the work phasor $W = -i\hat E\, e^{i\Psi}$ and the
polarization-basis current $\hat\jmath = u_x$ (planar), $(u_x - iu_y)/\sqrt2$
(helical):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unavgkick}
  \frac{d\gamma}{ds} = -\frac{\mathrm{Re}[W \bar{\hat\jmath}\,]}{u_s\, m_e},
  \qquad
  \mathrm{src} \mathrel{+}= i\,e^{-i\Psi}\,\hat\jmath\;
     \frac{Z_0\, c\,\delta}{2\,\Delta_{\mathrm{grid}}^2\,\Delta s}\,\frac{w}{u_s},
\end{equation}
the averaged deposit of Eq.~\ref{eq:source} with the coupling removed and the actual
quiver current in its place. The $1/u_s$ --- where the averaged solver carries
Genesis's $1/\gamma$ --- is a merit choice: kick and source are exact duals of one
wave equation, and using the same $u_s$ in both (same operands, same bilinear
weights, unitary diffraction between substeps) makes the coherent energy exchange
cancel in $E+U$ \emph{identically}, leaving only physical spontaneous emission and
rounding in the ledger; measured, this tightened the ledger 65$\times$ over the
$1/\gamma$ convention. The period-averaged limit shifts by
$O(1-\beta_\parallel) \sim 5\times10^{-9}$. Period-averaging the pair reproduces
\S\ref{sec:coupling} --- the $JJ$ Bessel factor emerges from the figure-8.

\emph{The energy ledger} (check zero, written per record step to
\code{<out\_root>.ledger.txt}): conservation of
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ledger}
  E_{\mathrm{beam}} + U + U_{\mathrm{esc}} - U_{\mathrm{sp}} + E_{\mathrm{rad}}, \qquad
  E_{\mathrm{beam}} = \sum_j w_j\,(\gamma_j - \gamma_0)\, m_e, \qquad
  U = \sum_{\mathrm{slices}} P\,\Delta s/c,
\end{equation}
with $E_{\mathrm{beam}}$ stored \emph{relative} to the reference so per-record
changes are not differenced off a large baseline at its own summation-rounding floor
(the two-pass-variance lesson of \S\ref{sec:numerics}), and $P$ the power of
\S\ref{sec:field}. The kick-side change is written alongside as an internal
cross-check. The last two columns make the time-dependent ledger close
\emph{exactly}: $U_{\mathrm{esc}}$ is the energy transmitted out of the window by
slippage, banked at each zero fill (the window is an open system --- light escapes
forward), and $U_{\mathrm{sp}}$ is the cumulative deposit energy
$\sum|\Delta E_{\mathrm{src}}|^2$ --- the one field-energy term the kick/deposit
duality does not charge to the beam (physically, the substep's spontaneous emission;
as in Genesis and MINERVA). Both vanish in steady state. $E_{\mathrm{rad}}$ is the energy the beam radiated away
under \code{bmad\_com}'s radiation switches (actual drawn sums, not expectations;
zero with the switches off, their default). Measured closure:
$8\times10^{-6}$ of turnover on the time-dependent harness configuration (checked at
$10^{-3}$), $1.0\times10^{-5}$ steady state. The closure premise --- radiation is the
beam's only exit channel --- is enforced by this mode's wake and space-charge
refusals; a wake would drain beam energy to the environment with no ledger column,
which is one more reason those refusals exist. The physical field of the
scalar envelope: planar $E_x = \mathrm{Re}[-i\hat E e^{i\Psi}]$; helical
$(E_x, E_y) = (\mathrm{Re}[-i\hat E e^{i\Psi}], -\mathrm{Re}[\hat E e^{i\Psi}])/\sqrt2$
--- both give intensity $|\hat E|^2/2Z_0$, so \S\ref{sec:field}'s power diagnostic is
mode-independent.

\emph{Spontaneous emission.} The classical rate is
$d\gamma/ds = \tfrac23 r_e \gamma^2 k_u^2 a_w^2$ ($a_w$ rms, both polarizations); Bmad's
own \code{radiation\_damping} through the same wiggler reproduces it to $10^{-4}$, and
it is the coefficient in Genesis's \code{Incoherent.cpp}. The two FEL modes stand in
very different relations to it. The averaged (KMR) mode \emph{emits} the radiation but
never debits the beam: its step adds $2S$ to the field while particles are kicked by
$E$, so the $4|S|^2$ part of the field energy is created (measured on a dark segment:
the field gains $134\times$ what the beam pays). Genesis's optional \code{\&sponrad}
module exists to add that loss by hand and is off by default. The unaveraged mode
conserves energy by construction (\S\ref{sec:unaveraged}'s $1/u_s$ deposit), so its
beam pays --- for the fraction of the emission the grid can hold, angles up to the FFT
Nyquist $\theta_{\max} = \lambda/2\,dx$. Measured at the benchmark parameters that is
3.3\% of the analytic rate, and the evidence that this is acceptance-limited undulator
radiation is its scaling: over a $16\times$ range in captured solid angle the loss
moves $0.84\% \to 11.4\%$, a measured $13.7\times$ against a predicted $10.5\times$.
Both modes now honor Bmad's GLOBAL switches
\code{bmad\_com\%radiation\_damping\_on} and
\code{\%radiation\_fluctuations\_on} --- the same switches every Bmad tracking path
honors (interludes get theirs through \code{track1}; this covers the custom-tracked
FEL step). Damping applies $d\gamma_j = -\tfrac23 r_e \gamma_j^2 k_u^2 a_w^2
\int g^2\,ds$ per record (each particle's own $\gamma$; the envelope integral, so
the unaveraged ramps radiate by their actual strength) --- measured: the averaged mode
lands on the analytic rate at $9\times10^{-4}$, and the unaveraged mode on the
ramp+capture composite $(1 - \tfrac{5}{4}l_{\mathrm{ramp}}/L) + f_{\mathrm{cap}}$
at $2\times10^{-6}$ (its grid-captured self-field work adds to the explicit term).
Fluctuations apply the standard quantum-diffusion variance
$1.015\times10^{-27} k_u^3 a_w^2 F(a_w)\gamma_0^4$ per meter (Genesis's
\code{Incoherent.cpp} fits of the Saldin form; Genesis reaches the same variance with

\subsection{Two polarizations}\label{sec:vector}

The radiation carries the envelope pair $(\hat E_x, \hat E_y)$ when any FEL element
is tilted (or the seed is y-polarized); otherwise $\hat E_y$ is never allocated and
the single-component path runs with its arithmetic untouched (every tier reproduces
bit for bit). Diffraction is polarization-diagonal; power and intensity are totals
$|\hat E_x|^2 + |\hat E_y|^2$. Each averaged element carries a polarization 2-vector
$\hat p$ --- planar with tilt $t$: $(\cos t, \sin t)$; helical: $(1, -i)/\sqrt2$ ---
its kick reads $E_{\mathrm{eff}} = \hat p^\dagger\!\cdot\!(\hat E_x,\hat E_y)$
and its deposit writes $\hat p\,S$, which reproduces the scalar convention exactly
when one polarization is live. The unaveraged mode needs no polarization vector at
all: the instantaneous kinetic momenta are real, and each component works against and
deposits into its own field component (the tilted element's field is the untilted
analytic potential evaluated in the rotated frame). The scalar helical envelope is
the co-rotating combination $(\hat E_x - i\hat E_y)/\sqrt2$; measured on dark
shot-noise runs the two representations agree at $7\times10^{-15}$.

\subsection{The field set: harmonic radiation}\label{sec:field-set}

The walk carries an ordered SET of radiation fields (\code{fel\_field\_struct}:
harmonic number, wavefront, slippage state, escape bank -- Genesis's
\code{vector<Field*>}), with the fundamental always entry~1: the ponderomotive
phase, the \code{phi0} advance and the slippage schedule are all defined against it.
A harmonic field $h$ carries wavelength $\lambda_1/h$ and enters the physics in
exactly three places: its coupling $f_c(h)$ (the Bessel factor of
\S\ref{sec:coupling}; zero for every harmonic of a helical device), the harmonic
ponderomotive phase $h\theta$ -- live per Runge--Kutta stage in the kick
(\code{BeamSolver::ODE}'s $\sum_i r_i e^{-ih_i\theta}$) and scaling the deposit
phase (\code{FieldSolver.cpp}'s $\theta_h = h\,\theta$) -- and its own diffraction
kernel and escape accounting at $\lambda_1/h$. In the V/m field
convention no other factor appears: Genesis's per-field $k_{s,h}$ factors in kick
and deposit are exactly its per-field internal unit conversion, which this
formulation absorbed once (\S\ref{sec:units}). Every field shares the time window,
so all slippage states advance in fundamental-wavelength units and the records
rotate in lockstep (one \code{Control::sample}); harmonic fields start dark and
grow from the bunching the beam carries, or import. A single-entry set is the
pre-harmonic walk, bit for bit -- the same overlay discipline as
\S\ref{sec:vector}, and the two are not yet validated together: harmonic fields
with two live polarizations, and with an unaveraged element (the unaveraged mode
carries the fundamental envelope only), are refused by name.

\subsection{Phasing between segments}\label{sec:phasing}

What the beam-field phase does between undulator segments, measured before it was
built (every claim below is a scan in \code{check\_phasing.py}). In the RELATIVE
mode -- the default, \code{absolute\_time\_tracking} false -- segments are
autophased: scanning an inter-segment gap by fractions of $2\gamma^2\lambda$ (one
full turn of drift slip, 25.8 mm at the benchmark) leaves the
bunching phase entering the next segment FLAT, measured on Genesis and on this code
alike, both interlude models. The mechanism is the co-moving reference convention:
\code{fel\_phi0\_rate} tracks the beam through a drift, and the window rotations are
whole wavelengths, carrier-neutral -- the fractional beam-vs-light slip
$k_s L/(2\gamma^2)$ never reaches the coupling. (Genesis's \code{calcSlippage}
carries commented-out code that would have applied that fraction to $\theta$;
the active code re-anchors, as the measurement shows.)

The deliberate off-phase knob is the wiggler's own \code{z\_offset} -- standard
Bmad misalignment, girder-composed, no new element or attribute. The autophase
anchor is the element's NOMINAL reference position, so a physical displacement
$\delta$ adds the real, never-re-anchored entry phase
$\Delta\phi = -2\pi\,\delta/(2\gamma^2\lambda)$, returned at exit (the downstream
break is shorter by the same $\delta$; downstream elements stay anchored). Measured
against the analytic slope, and against GENESIS'S OWN
\code{PHASESHIFTER} element scanned over $\phi = 2\pi\,\delta/(2\gamma^2\lambda)$:
the two bunching-phase curves agree at $1.9\times10^{-8}$ and the exit POWER against
$\phi$ at $9.3\times10^{-9}$, both codes tracking the same Genesis-written initial
dumps -- so the phase reaches the physics identically, not just the bookkeeping. (On
independently loaded beams the same power comparison sits at $1.4\times10^{-4}$,
which measures the two loaders; hence the shared start.) The sign is Genesis's
``delay goes backwards'', $\theta \to \theta - \phi$, applied persistently -- and
note Genesis's phase shifter must have FINITE LENGTH to register at all; a
zero-length one silently does nothing.

In the ABSOLUTE mode -- \code{bmad\_com[absolute\_time\_tracking] = T} in the
lattice, honored per element through Bmad's own resolver, never a namelist knob --
the walk keeps the real carrier phase of every break:
$\phi_0 \mathrel{-}= 2\pi L/(2\gamma^2\lambda)$ per interlude element, no floors
(whole turns wrap). Inter-segment phasing then follows the real spacings, the
recirculating-linac discipline: a wrong-length break visibly detunes the next
segment, and moving an element moves its phase -- the absolute-mode gap scan
reproduces the relative-mode \code{z\_offset} scan point by point (cross-mode
identity).

CHICANES: a break whose elements bend the reference (sbends, patches) detours the
beam while the radiation goes straight, so the light's path is the CHORD between
the flanking undulator faces -- computed from \code{ele\%floor}, never entered by
hand -- while \code{vec(5)} is measured against the reference arc. The
arc-minus-chord delay is charged as whole-wavelength window rotations on the
break's last element (Genesis's chicane semantics: ``always autophasing'') plus,
in absolute mode, its carrier phase; the light drift is shortened by the same
delay. Relative mode drops the geometric fraction (measured flat under an angle
scan); absolute mode ramps at exactly the slope an independent 2D geometric
computation of $d(\mathrm{arc}-\mathrm{chord})/d\alpha$ predicts. Only a CLOSED
BUMP keeps the light on the next undulator's axis: floor orientations and the
chord direction are asserted, anything else refused by name, as are bends and
patches under the genesis-model interludes (Genesis's drift/quad set cannot
represent them) and a \code{z\_offset} that exceeds its upstream break. The
unaveraged energy ledger closes across a chicane sandwich. The window ROTATIONS the
delay buys are checked time-dependently (steady state never exercises them): a
chicane banks exactly $\lfloor \mathrm{delay}/\lambda \rfloor$ more escaped slices
than its arc-matched straight twin, at three delays, and the run is byte-identical
at 1 and 8 threads. Delays for that check are chosen MID-INTERVAL: a target of
exactly $3.000\lambda$ lands on the floor boundary, where the $6\times10^{-8}$
difference between the exact trace and the small-angle form decides between 2 and 3
-- the boundary was located at $2.99 \to 2$, $3.01 \to 3$, which independently
confirms the walk's floor-derived delay against the trace at that scale.

\section{Numerical practices}\label{sec:numerics}

\emph{Two-pass variance.} All second moments subtract the mean before squaring: for
$\gamma \sim 10^4$ with $\sigma \sim 10^{-3}$ the one-pass
$\langle v^2\rangle - \langle v\rangle^2$ form loses the entire $\sigma$ to
cancellation (hidden for five deliverables until a uniform wake kick first moved the
mean).

\emph{Constants floors.} Comparisons against Genesis bottom out at the mismatch of
its truncated constants, per term: $Z_0 = 376.73$ ($8.3\times10^{-7}$, the FEL core
and resistive/geometric wakes), $\varepsilon_0 = 8.85\times10^{-12}$ in the
long-range space charge ($4.7\times10^{-4}$), $e = 1.6\times10^{-19}$ and
$\varepsilon_0 = 8.854\times10^{-12}$ in the roughness coefficient
($1.4\times10^{-3}$ of that kernel). Each tier's tolerance is sized to the floor of
the terms it enables.

\emph{Step size.} Measured convergence (one shared 32-slice dump at
$\code{sample}=12$, \code{ds\_step} swept): roughly first order in the exponential
gain region; saturation power holds to $\sim$3\% up to six periods per step and
misses by 26\% at twelve. Two to three periods per step is the operating point;
SIMPLEX's twelve-period economy belongs to its semianalytic solver and does not
transfer.

\emph{Ulp accounting.} The $\gamma \leftrightarrow p_z$ round trip costs $\sim$1 ulp
of $\gamma$ per step (it moved tier1 from $2.5\times10^{-12}$ to
$2.8\times10^{-11}$ at transcription level); a lattice-attribute round trip
($a_w$ through \code{b\_max}) costs 1 ulp amplified to $3.4\times10^{-12}$ over the
line; moving verbatim code between compilation units shifts arithmetic 1--2 ulp
(gain-amplified to $1.2\times10^{-4}$ of bunching phase). Bit-identity anchors hold
within one compilation unit; across moves, anchor at ulp tolerance and record the
drift.

\emph{Thread safety.} Cross-slice state changes only at serial points: the FFTW plan
cache is threadprivate, the $K_2$ kernel and its step propagator $e^{K_2\delta z}$
build serially before parallel loops and are read-only inside them, source
accumulators are per call, and slippage/migration/wake updates run at barriers. Each slice's arithmetic is independent of which thread runs
it, so results are bit-identical across thread counts --- a checked property, and the
only instrument that catches shared-accumulator races (invisible to every
single-threaded check).

\begin{validation}
FINDINGS.md 4.8, 7.9, 7.10, 7.14, 7.18; the coarse-step table in the README; the
thread check and its shared-accumulator mutation (7.0 relative between thread counts,
bit-identical single-threaded).
\end{validation}


\section{The program}\label{sec:program}

The tracker is laid out the way Tao is laid out: the structs in one file, the
namelist layer quarantined, the work in library modules, and a driver that is
read-parse-call. Every user-visible input struct lives in \texttt{fel\_struct}
(defaults in the declarations); \texttt{fel\_input\_mod} reads THREE namelist
groups from one input file, each setting structs directly, Tao's
\texttt{\&tao\_params} pattern:

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{\&fel\_params}: \texttt{lat\_file}, the \texttt{global\%...}
  run switches, Bmad's own \texttt{bmad\_com} and \texttt{space\_charge\_com}
  set directly (the old \texttt{radiation\_damping} / \texttt{radiation\_fluctuations}
  scalars retired with this), and the \texttt{wake\%} / \texttt{sc\%} collective
  descriptions.
\item \texttt{\&fel\_beam\_init}: Bmad's \texttt{beam\_init\%...} bunch
  description, the \texttt{imp\%...} resampler, source/output files and the
  beam-side check instruments.
\item \texttt{\&fel\_wavefront\_init}: \texttt{wavefront\_init\%...}, the
  \texttt{beam\_init} analog for radiation -- the field record IS the time
  window, so the window lives here -- plus \texttt{field\_file} imports.
\end{itemize}

The retired flat \texttt{\&fel\_track\_params} group is refused by name, the
error mapping each parameter found in it to its new home.

The work is four library modules -- \texttt{fel\_setup\_mod} (recognition,
refusals, the slippage/autophasing schedule, geometry breaks, diagnostics
setup), \texttt{fel\_init\_mod} (beam and wavefront starting states),
\texttt{fel\_track\_line\_mod} (\texttt{track\_fel\_line(run)}, the walk) and
\texttt{fel\_io\_mod} (dumps, the escaped bank, the pulse, the stats finalize)
-- all operating on one explicit \texttt{fel\_run\_struct} (no singleton: an
oscillator or scan driver wants several passes over one state). The library
contract: nothing in these modules stops; every error returns through
\texttt{err\_flag} and the program decides. \texttt{track\_fel\_line} is
re-entrant -- twice in one process is bit-identical to two processes (checked)
-- and \texttt{tests/lucifer\_smoke\_test.f90} drives the whole library with no
namelist anywhere, reproducing a namelist run dataset-identically.

All terminal output goes through Bmad's \texttt{out\_io} (never \texttt{print}
in the library), which fixes the message contract scripts parse: refusals are
\texttt{[ERROR]}/\texttt{[FATAL]}-tagged blocks naming the offending input in
capitals, notes are \texttt{[INFO]}/\texttt{[WARNING]} blocks, and machine-read
data lines (the import moments and per-slice currents, the \texttt{progress:}
lines, the written-file listing) are emitted untagged and unindented at full
precision, so a parser matches the line's own leading text.

\subsection{The comb}\label{sec:comb}

\texttt{global\%comb\_ds\_save} is Bmad's \texttt{bunch\_track\_struct\%ds\_save}
name and semantics verbatim (Tao's \texttt{comb\_ds\_save}; ``$< 0$ $\Rightarrow$
no comb calculated''): negative keeps NO per-record rows at all (the
\texttt{element\_end/} arrays, dumps and finals remain -- with no records the
element-end moments are taken by one parallel sweep over slices instead of being
read from the current record); zero (the default) records every
position, bit-for-bit today's file; positive records a row when $z$ has
advanced \texttt{comb\_ds\_save} past the last one, element ends always. The
one deliberate deviation from Tao is the default (0 where Tao uses $-1$):
our per-record arrays are the primary stats contract where Tao's comb is an
optional extra. The rule lives in ONE place (\texttt{fel\_comb\_take});
the setup's record-count precompute replays it with the walk's own $z$
arithmetic, so the stats arrays are exact-sized in every mode. The
\texttt{diag} file and the unaveraged ledger follow the comb.

Either way the element-end row for the WHOLE WINDOW is assembled from the per-slice
moments by the pooled-covariance identity,
\begin{equation}
  m = \frac{\sum_s w_s m_s}{\sum_s w_s}, \qquad
  S = \frac{\sum_s w_s \left[ S_s + (m_s - m)(m_s - m)^{\mathsf T} \right]}{\sum_s w_s},
  \label{eq:pool}
\end{equation}
never by concatenating the window's particles into one bunch. The between-group term
is what makes \eqref{eq:pool} exact rather than an average of covariances. Each slice
enters moved from its local $z$ chart to the global window chart; because
\texttt{fel\_concat\_slices} places a particle at $z_{\rm global} = z_{\rm local} +
\beta (i_s - 1)\Delta$ with the particle's own $\beta$, that move is a SHEAR of
$(z, p_z)$ once $\beta$ is linearized about the slice's mean $p_z$, and the pool
applies it as $S \to J S J^{\mathsf T}$ with the single off-diagonal
$J_{56} = (i_s - 1)\Delta \, {\rm d}\beta/{\rm d}p_z$. The residual is second order
in that expansion.

\begin{validation}
check\_diagnostics reproduces \eqref{eq:pool} and its shear independently and holds
the stored row to it, and confirms the between-group term is load-bearing (dropping it
moves the row by order unity). Against the particle sum it replaced: worst relative
4.0e-12 over every whole-window quantity on that configuration, 5.0e-11 over the
96-slice SASE example's 48 element ends. It removed 110 million single-threaded
particle visits from a 131-slice run (16.5\% of its wall clock).
\end{validation}

\subsection{The tracking window}\label{sec:window}

\texttt{global\%track\_start} / \texttt{track\_end} (Tao's names; Genesis's
\texttt{zstop} parity) are element locators bounding the walk. The schedule --
slippage, autophasing, break geometry, including the end-of-lattice fixup --
is always built on the FULL lattice, so a windowed run composes exactly with
the full one: $[{\rm start}, D]$ then $[{\rm after}\ D, {\rm end}]$ from its
dumps reproduces the one-shot run to the dump format's own round-trip floor
(measured $3\times10^{-13}$; the walk itself is bit-for-bit, and a windowed
run's finals equal the full run's mid-line dumps exactly).

\subsection{Provenance}\label{sec:meta}

\texttt{stats.h5} carries a \texttt{Meta/} group (Genesis parity: its

\subsection{The coherent source (SIMPLEX hybrid)}\label{sec:coherent-source}

\texttt{global\%source\_model = 'coherent'} replaces the per-particle source deposit
with Tanaka's coherent retrieval (PRAB \textbf{27}, 030703 (2024); implemented from
the paper -- SIMPLEX's own source is unlicensed): the slice bunch factor splits
$b = b_C + b_S$, the spatially incoherent $b_S$ -- whose radiation diffracts away,
and whose per-cell sampling noise is the artifact that makes under-populated runs
overestimate gain -- is dropped, and $b_C$ deposits as one analytic Gaussian carrying
the slice's exact source phasor $S$ (the deposit's own normalization contract,
$\sum \mathrm{crsource} = S$). The width is $\kappa\,\sigma$ with $\kappa$ from the
order-0/1 Laguerre-Gauss sums (the paper's Eq.~26), charge-weighted across the window
so near-empty edge slices cannot poison the fit; the centering and the full
$2\times2$ second-moment matrix (tilt included) are this port's extension, letting
offset and mismatched -- but transversely Gaussian -- beams through. Everything else
is untouched: the grid, the FFT diffraction, slippage, banking, the stats file, and
the per-particle gather.

Measured on the seeded 6\,GeV lethargy configuration (the regime where the artifact
bites hardest): the plain deposit at $M=128$ per slice fakes $\ln P$ by $+0.42$
where the $M=8192$ reference absorbs; the coherent source at the same $M=128$ stays
within $0.05$ -- a demonstrated $64\times$ particle reduction at the model's own bias,
which is $1.9\times10^{-2}$ in $|\ln P|$ at large $M$. Guards, all by name: a
per-slice excess-kurtosis test against its sampling significance
($\sqrt{24/m_\mathrm{ind}}$), charge-gated so sparse edge slices are exempt; refusals
for the unaveraged mode, harmonics (even harmonics are invalid in the method itself),
two polarizations, and -- measured, a $\sim$175$\times$ startup deficit -- dark
starts: spontaneous, spatially incoherent emission dominates SASE startup and is
exactly what the coherent model drops, so seeded runs only. The related SIMPLEX
coarse-stepping observation was priced separately: 12 undulator periods per step
costs $2.6\times10^{-3}$ in $|\ln P|$ on the same configuration (taper and harmonics
not yet tested at coarse steps).
